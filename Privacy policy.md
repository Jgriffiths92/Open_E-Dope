Open E-Dope — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

1. Introduction

Open E-Dope ("the App") is a ballistic calculation and data display application developed by the Open E-Dope project.

This Privacy Policy explains how the App accesses, processes, stores and shares information when you use it, including when connecting to compatible external devices.

The privacy of users is important to this project. This policy is intended to explain what information the App handles and how it is used.

2. Information the App Processes

Depending on the features you use, Open E-Dope may process the following information on your device:

- Ballistic and shooting data: Range measurements, ballistic calculations, environmental conditions, elevation and wind corrections, and related calculation inputs.
- Device connection information: Bluetooth connection status and information required to communicate with compatible devices, such as supported Kestrel devices.
- Display data: Information formatted for transfer to compatible NFC-powered electronic paper displays.
- App settings and configuration: Preferences and other settings required for the App to operate.

These categories describe information used by the App's functionality. They do not necessarily mean that the information is collected by the developer or transmitted outside your device.

3. How Information Is Used

Information processed by the App is used to:

- Calculate and display ballistic solutions.
- Communicate with compatible Bluetooth devices.
- Transfer selected information to compatible external displays.
- Maintain the App's settings and provide its intended functionality.
- Diagnose technical problems, if diagnostic features are implemented.

The App is not intended to use your ballistic data for advertising or sell it to third parties.

4. Data Collection and Transmission

The App is designed to support local processing and communication with compatible external hardware.

Where information is processed locally, it remains on your device unless you choose to transfer it elsewhere or a feature explicitly transmits it.

Bluetooth communication may transfer information between the App and a compatible device. NFC communication may transfer selected display data to a compatible NFC-powered electronic paper display.

The developer does not receive your ballistic calculations, range data, or device information merely because you use the App. Any additional data collection, including through third-party libraries or services, will be disclosed as applicable.

5. Data Storage and Retention

Information may be stored locally on your device where required by the App's features.

Locally stored information remains subject to your device's storage, backup and security settings. You may be able to remove it by using the App's available controls or clearing the App's data or uninstalling the App, subject to your device's behaviour and any external backups.

The App does not provide a developer-hosted user account or cloud storage service under the functionality described in this policy.

6. Permissions

Open E-Dope may request Android permissions necessary for its features, which may include Bluetooth permissions and NFC access.

On some Android versions, Bluetooth scanning or connection workflows may be subject to additional system permission requirements.

Permissions are used to support the relevant functionality. The App's actual permission requests depend on the Android version and features included in the installed release.

7. Third-Party Services

The App may rely on third-party software libraries or Android platform services to provide its functionality.

These components may process information according to their own technical requirements and privacy policies. Where a third-party component collects or transmits user data, that handling will be disclosed in accordance with applicable requirements.

Open E-Dope does not authorise the sale of users' personal information for advertising purposes.

8. Data Security

Reasonable measures are taken in the App's design to minimise unnecessary data handling.

No method of electronic storage or data transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users should protect their devices and exercise care when transferring data to external hardware or other applications.

9. Children's Privacy

Open E-Dope is not designed specifically for children. The developer does not knowingly collect children's personal information for the purpose of operating the App.

If the App's functionality or intended audience changes, this section will be updated as necessary.

10. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights concerning personal information associated with you.

Because the App's described functionality primarily involves local processing and device-to-device communication, the developer may not hold a copy of the information processed on your device.

For privacy enquiries, questions about data handling, or requests concerning information held by the developer, please use the contact details below.

11. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the App's features, permissions, data practices or legal requirements change.

The latest version will be published at the public URL associated with the App's Google Play listing.
